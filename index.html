AI Network · LUNA-X orchestrator

LUNA-X is not one AI. It routes each task to the best connected model and fuses their answers. Keys stay in this browser (optional “remember”) and go only to that provider. Free options are marked FREE.

Routing: Claude runs LUNA's reasoning, tools, safety checks and SELENE deep scans. Gemini, OpenAI and Llama independently verify each detection, and LUNA-X fuses the votes. With no Claude key, LUNA answers commands offline and uses any connected free AI for conversation.
